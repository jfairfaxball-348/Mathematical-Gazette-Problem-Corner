## Final submitted formulation

A road runs through more than two villages, all at different altitudes. A surviving village may be invaded and razed by the surviving villages immediately before and after it along the road whenever its altitude lies strictly between theirs. After it is razed, its attackers become neighbours. The question is whether the eventual survivors depend on attack order and which villages ultimately survive.

The answer remains: the two endpoint villages and exactly the original local peaks and valleys.
