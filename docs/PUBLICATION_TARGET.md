# Publication Target: The Mathematical Gazette — Problem Corner

**Verified:** 2026-10-05  
**Scope:** Stage-1 factual orientation only. This is not the Stage-2 historical corpus analysis.

## Current journal facts

- **Journal:** *The Mathematical Gazette*.
- **Section:** **Problem Corner**.
- **Current host/publishing platform:** Taylor & Francis Online.
- **Publishing relationship:** Taylor & Francis states that the journal is published on behalf of the **Association for Mathematics in Education (AMiE)**; the journal information page describes the Gazette as published by AMiE.
- **Current Gazette editor:** Dr Peter Rowlett, Sheffield Hallam University.
- **Current Problem Corner editor:** **Chris Starr**, Kirkbie Kendal School.
- **Publication frequency:** **3 issues per year**.
- **Identifiers:** Print ISSN **0025-5572**; Online ISSN **2056-6328**.
- **Access model:** print and online journal; Taylor & Francis describes the journal as hybrid open access / Open Select.
- **Level/readership:** the current aims and scope describe a global audience including teachers, students, academic mathematicians and other interested readers, with a guide level from upper secondary school through early-stage university.

The 2026 move matters when researching archives: Cambridge Core remains an important legacy host for material through 2025, while current publication is on Taylor & Francis Online.

## Current Problem Corner proposal instructions

The most recent Problem Corner page checked (published online 20 August 2026) says:

- solutions to set problems should preferably be emailed to **Chris Starr**;
- postal correspondence may be addressed to **Problem Corner, c/o Association for Mathematics in Education, Charnwood Building, Holywell Park, Loughborough University Science and Enterprise Park, Leicestershire, LE11 3AQ**;
- **proposals for problems are equally welcome**;
- proposals should also be sent to Chris Starr;
- proposals should be accompanied by **solutions and any relevant background information**.

Current email shown in the section: **czqstarr@gmail.com**.

The deadline printed in each Problem Corner is a deadline for **reader solutions to the currently posed problems**. It should not be treated as a stated deadline for new problem proposals unless the current instructions explicitly say so.

## Distinguish two submission flows

1. **Proposing a new Problem Corner problem:** send the proposal to the Problem Corner editor with a solution and relevant background information.
2. **Submitting a reader solution to a published problem:** follow the deadline and addressing instructions printed in the relevant Problem Corner.

This project concerns flow 1.
