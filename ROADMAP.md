# Frozen Roadmap

This five-stage programme is authoritative. An agent may refine execution details within a stage but may not reorder, merge, skip, or redefine stages without explicit human instruction and a logged project decision.

## Stage 1 — Scaffold / Bootstrap

Set up repository structure, charter, authority, workflow, gates, research schemas, publication-target facts, and success criteria.

**Exit:** the Stage-1 gate in `authoritative/STAGE_GATE_LEDGER.md` passes.

## Stage 2 — Research

Review a substantial corpus of existing Mathematical Gazette Problem Corner problems **and published solutions**.

Target at least roughly **60 problems**, preferably **80–100**, spanning multiple years, issues, and mathematical types where accessible.

The purpose is analytical, not merely bibliographic. Derive an evidence-based specification of:

- statement length and economy;
- accessibility and prerequisites;
- mathematical depth;
- types and sources of surprise;
- common underlying mechanisms;
- qualitative/everyday framing;
- solution methods, style, and length;
- whether multiple solutions are featured;
- recurring themes;
- differences from olympiad exercises, textbook exercises, and internet puzzles;
- features that appear to make a problem attractive for publication.

**Hard rule:** Stage 2 must complete before serious candidate invention begins.

## Stage 3 — Discovery

Generate a broad, diverse pool of up to roughly **50 serious candidates** based on Stage-2 evidence. Do not pad the list.

Each serious candidate must use the candidate schema and have a plausible, checked mathematical kernel and solution route. Track diversity so cosmetic variants do not inflate the pool.

## Stage 4 — Prior-art / Novelty Audit

Audit **all serious Stage-3 candidates**.

Search both:

1. visible/story formulations; and
2. mathematically equivalent formulations.

Use multiple source classes appropriate to the problem. Final classifications are:

- `CLEAR`
- `CLEAR_WITH_RELATED_PRIOR_ART`
- `MATERIAL_PRIOR_ART_POSSIBLY_SALVAGEABLE`
- `REDISCOVERY`
- `TOO_CLOSE_TO_EXISTING_PROBLEM`
- `NOVELTY_UNCERTAIN`

Only after this audit choose a primary candidate and a small number of reserves.

## Stage 5 — Formalisation and Submission Package

For the selected candidate:

- freeze the canonical problem statement;
- produce a complete rigorous solution;
- conduct adversarial checking and edge cases;
- add an alternative solution where useful;
- conduct reader testing if practical;
- perform a final prior-art audit on the exact finished formulation;
- prepare relevant mathematical/background notes;
- prepare the Problem Corner submission package;
- draft a concise submission email to the current Problem Corner editor.

Formalisation means mathematical precision, not automatic use of Lean or another proof assistant.

**End state:** a publication-ready package. Sending remains outside the autonomous project unless explicitly requested.
